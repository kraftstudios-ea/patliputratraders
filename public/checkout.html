About taxes

All poster and product prices are inclusive of 18% GST. The consolidated GST shown on your order is already included in the item subtotal and is never charged as an extra fee.

Depending on your delivery address, statutory tax is accounted as CGST and SGST (within Bihar) or as IGST (across other states). Only offers, discounts, handling, and delivery fees are applied to calculate the final payable amount. Your tax invoice shows the complete breakdown.
