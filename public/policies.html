Shipping & Delivery

This policy covers how your order is curated, quality-inspected, packed and delivered. Patliputra Traders ensures every chandelier, wall lamp, fan, and architectural fixture undergoes rigorous electrical and physical inspection before dispatch.

Where Your Order Is Dispatched

Every lighting fixture is handled and dispatched from our primary showroom and distribution hub in Patna, Bihar. Express delivery is available across Patna and nearby districts, while pan-India shipments are handled via priority air and surface logistics.

Serviceable Locations

We deliver to any PIN code served by our courier partners across India. In the rare case that your PIN code is not serviceable, we will contact you promptly and refund you in full. For bulk architect or builder orders to custom project sites, write to us at patliputratraders01@gmail.com or WhatsApp us directly.

Processing & Dispatch

In-stock fixtures are tested, safely packaged with shock-absorbing foam/bubble wrap, and dispatched within 1 to 2 working days. Custom chandelier assemblies or large profile runs may take 2 to 4 working days.

Estimated Delivery Timelines

  • Patna & Bihar: 2 to 4 working days.
  • Delhi NCR & Metros: 4 to 7 working days.
  • Rest of India: 6 to 10 working days.

Delivery Charges

  • Standard insured delivery is a flat ₹59.
  • Delivery is free on orders with a cart value of ₹599 or more.

100% Transit Break-Free Guarantee

Lighting products contain delicate elements such as crystals, blown glass, and optical lenses. Every package is sealed in heavy-duty multi-layer crating. If any component arrives damaged, we replace the broken part or entire fixture free of charge with no hassle.

Cancellation & Replacements

You can cancel your order free of charge at any time before dispatch. Once dispatched, tracking is generated and the package cannot be cancelled in transit.

If any item arrives damaged or non-functional, email us at patliputratraders01@gmail.com within 48 hours of delivery along with photos and an unboxing video. We will promptly dispatch a replacement part or fixture.

Refunds & Claims

Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment source within 5 to 7 working days. For COD or direct bank transfers, refunds are credited directly to your bank account via NEFT/UPI.

Privacy Policy

Patliputra Traders operates this platform to offer a seamless lighting showroom experience. We respect your privacy and never sell or rent your personal information to third parties.
