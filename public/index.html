Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our lighting fixtures & ordering

What color temperature (CCT) should I choose?

For cozy living rooms, dining spaces, and bedrooms, 3000K Warm White creates an inviting, luxurious ambiance. For functional areas like kitchens, vanities, and study desks, 4000K Natural White provides balanced daylight clarity. Many of our fixtures come with built-in 3-in-1 color switching.

How are fragile crystal chandeliers shipped?

All delicate chandeliers and glass pendant lights are packed inside high-density multi-layered foam and reinforced cartons (with wooden crate reinforcement on large models). In case of any transit issue, we replace the item or part immediately.

Do you offer bulk contractor and architect discounts?

Yes! We provide tiered B2B wholesale pricing for architects, electrical contractors, hotels, and builders. Contact our team at +91-9234755686 or via WhatsApp for project quotations.

Patliputra Traders is located in Patna, Bihar. You can connect with our showroom team for on-site consultation or visit our digital showroom on Instagram at @patliputratraderpatna.
